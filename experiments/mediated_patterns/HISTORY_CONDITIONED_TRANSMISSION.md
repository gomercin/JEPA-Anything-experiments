# History-conditioned transmission: write, wait, probe

**Outcome:** a brief supplied write produced a slow, translation-like configuration
change and a different later source response on all three fresh preparations.
This is finite configurational retention over waits 50–100, with continuing
relaxation and no claim of permanent memory. The ordinary history-specific
tangent predicted the fresh nonlinear responses. Complete results follow the
preserved prospective decisions below.

Prospective living note, 2026-09-26. Start at merged 44e44a9. No later local
history-conditioned attempt was found. Prior reports, reductions, source
snapshots, gains and checkpoint protections remain unchanged.

Question: from identical complete prepared A/B/C fields, can one compact write
at B alter the later response to the identical fixed A probe, after the write's
fast deformation has relaxed? This is an accessibility experiment under a
supplied actuator, not learning or spontaneous interface production.

The unforced laws are gradient relaxation. A weak amplitude perturbation can
wash out; a displacement-like direction may be slow because translation of an
isolated solution has no restoring force in a homogeneous domain. The triple
is interacting, so neither an exact neutral mode nor indefinite memory follows.
Use a cheap finite-write continuation before committing to probe comparisons.

**Preparation and bounds before screening.** Original coefficients; L=192,
N=768, dt=.00625. A=-28, B=-4, C=28; independently relax components 150, reuse
the existing .02 residual recipe, then freely relax the assembled triple 40.
B=-4 avoids a parity-degenerate centered assay; it is chosen without fresh
outcomes. All histories of a seed branch from the same complete initial arrays.
No movement setter, donor transplant, recentering, pinning or ongoing drive.
Require all measured gaps >22, A/C offsets <.5, B offset <.75, window mass
between 4 and 12, width between 2 and 4, and |u|<.25 outside radius-10 component
neighborhoods. Check histories throughout and probe endpoints; all sampled
probe branch masses/centroids are checked too. These finite observation bounds
are a regime screen, not a mathematical classification of all possible splitting.

**Pilot family.** Exactly two radius-8 fixed profiles at B: smooth compact bump
multiplied by cos(x-B) or sin(x-B), individually unit spatial L2. Add .4*q to u
at t=0, leave m unchanged. This supplied instantaneous write ends immediately.
Its support is disjoint from A/C. Initially examine waits 10,50,100 and unprobed
continuation through 180. No fresh seed is opened during this choice. One or
two justified bounded revisions are permitted, recorded here before use.

**Physical assay.** Later source P is the existing fixed unit-L2 amplitude
profile at A, additive epsilon=.02, with post-probe horizon 80. Fixed smooth C
mass and signed moment remain primary; mean mediator and A/B readouts are
diagnostics. For every wait, branch appropriately aged states without time
reset or new preparation. R0=y01-y00, RW=y11-y10, Delta_R=RW-R0. Never use
y11-y00 as transmission. Delays share ancestry and are not independent seeds.
Record actual write/probe norms, energy/source jumps, geometry, mediator and
fixed-anchor readouts. Equal probe norm is not equal work; do not rescale.

**Relaxation diagnosis.** Track write-only C output, full-field delta-u/delta-m
norms, B's center/width/mass, and a read-only projection of local delta-u onto
-minus the current unwritten spatial derivative. Save displacement coefficient
and remaining shape norm without feeding either back into the simulation.
This can identify a translation-like carrier without asserting a physical mode.
A persistent configuration difference is an ordinary present-state difference,
not an extra history variable. Two late waits cannot establish permanent memory.
No practical binary receiver threshold will be invented.

**Response explanation and attribution.** Validate the .02 and .01 A probes
against tangents along each history's OWN unprobed continuation. A common
first-order history would erase cross-pulse interaction by construction and is
not the comparator. If a late effect is resolved, use the existing stage-matched
B feedback cut only after the probe boundary, fixed core/taper radii 8/12 at
B=-4, independently replaying each history's own no-probe sham. QW=RW_full-RW_cut,
Q0=R0_full-R0_cut; compare Delta_Q. Retain source law, transport and direct u
coupling. Monitor A; any loop response prevents one-pass attribution. This
interruption concerns expression, not where the alteration was stored or created.

**Qualification and freeze.** Development seed 8101 is already exposed historical
material, freshly prepared here. Fresh seeds 10101/10102/10103 are whole unseen
preparations, paired across histories. Select write and waits from the pilot,
then freeze numerical floors, interpretation rules and analysis before fresh

and cut response refinement RMS errors across BOTH histories, over all waits

retain direct contrast refinement without using favorable cancellation instead.
Do not inherit the previous floor. No new practical gate is required for a
numerically detectable finite-lifetime result; retention criteria follow the
observed development relaxation and freeze before fresh evaluation.

Prospective consequence: each fresh history's saved own-sham tangent predicts
its later .02 response before nonlinear reference access. Report per-readout
absolute and normalized residuals; provisional gates 2% total response, 10%
for the smaller resolved history contrast (or error below its frozen floor).
The predictor receives full unprobed current state and future sham trajectory;
it is diagnostic, not deployable autonomous prediction. Preserve failures.

## Fresh outcome and stopping point

Source/freeze `945c2ff`; fresh preparations 10101, 10102, 10103. The same initial
fields branch into the two histories; each is probed at waits 10, 50, 100 with
post-probe horizon 80. All late-retention and prospective prediction checks
pass. No fresh case or delay was discarded and no design revision followed
fresh access. The earlier even-write pilot relaxed much more strongly and was
not assayed further; that is not a general washout result for all even writes.

These percentages describe the RMS **curve difference**, not amplitude loss.
At wait 100, total mass-response RMS falls 5.25–5.37%, and moment-response RMS
falls 13.33–13.59%. Descriptive per-readout gains are .9443–.9457 and
.8594–.8627. Even after those gains, residuals are 6.19–6.54% and 9.63–10.44%
of the written response. Thus there is attenuation plus a curve change beyond
scalar gain. Delay versus other shape changes is not uniquely separated; one
source direction cannot establish input selectivity or a new channel.

The curve difference grows between late waits: mass Delta_R RMS by 1.395–1.408x,
moment by 1.288x. Meanwhile the translation projection retains 95.3% of its
wait-50 value. At time 100 B's centroid differs by .08829–.08838, local translation
projection by .12346–.12366, and the remaining local shape residual is .02093–
.02100 in L2. B's initial mass excess has mostly relaxed to .00484–.00498; small
width/source differences remain. A has also shifted relative to its unwritten
history by .01085–.01090, and C by about −.00057. This is a changed **coupled
configuration**, largely translation-like near B, not proof that B's position
alone stores the effect. Unprobed differences continue slowly decaying through
180; no new fixed point, bistability or indefinite memory is demonstrated.

The mediator difference at time 100 has L2 .01447–.01449. Propagating only the
recorded time-50 mediator difference with the exact homogeneous spectral
operator leaves just 2.053–2.058e-5; the remaining .01447–.01449 is associated
with ongoing different u-sourcing. This rules out passive inherited mediator
residue as the whole explanation on that interval. The residual-source term
is conditional on the coupled trajectories, not an independent causal fraction.
The write-only C mass difference remains about .000616, far larger than the
probe responses: correct four-branch subtraction is essential.

**Responsive expression.** At wait 100, Q0 mass RMS is 1.676–1.684e-8 and QW is
1.724–1.733e-8. Their **difference**, Delta_Q, resolves at 8.095–8.107e-10 for
mass and 3.658–3.715e-10 for moment. All three waits resolve. Most of the history
contrast survives this selected cut; its smaller time-dependent change is
neither a sum of independent causal fractions nor unique localization of
storage. The cut's A-exterior mediator response has RMS 3.79–3.84e-7, confirming
loop-wide expression dependence. Background conditioning, source dynamics,
direct u coupling, mediator transport and C's own dynamics remain intact.

**Ordinary explanation and prospective check.** The complete present fields
and each history's own future unprobed continuation suffice for the interacting
ETDRK4 tangent to predict the new .02 probe response. All 72 recorded fresh
checks pass their frozen gates. Worst C total-response relative RMS error is

**Physical input budget.** Writes have L2 .4, source increments .18467–.20700

**Numerical and state envelope.** New full-history half-dt/double-N refinement

Floors use separate response errors before subtraction. Maximum checkpoint

**Supplied and unearned.** The SH35-plus-mediator laws, separated component
preparation, B=-4 assembly, physical write/probe actuators and readouts are
supplied. The later field evolution is unconstrained. This establishes access
to a finite changed-response configuration, not learning from A, spontaneous
