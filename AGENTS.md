# JEPA-Anything-experiments — Agent Guide

## Question continuity and learning from results

Use the [Atlas routing guide](https://github.com/gomercin/motif-atlas/blob/main/maps/roadmap/routing-guide.md#question-continuity-and-learning-from-results) and the relevant current owner when choosing or interpreting research. Read enough history to recover the question and its corrections; do not exhaust a backlog first.

In the existing issue, script, notebook or report, keep a compact account of the **question → tested construction → comparison → observation/lesson → decision**. No additional form, audit or approval round is required. Separate the original intuition from its representation, implementation and measure. If the requested construction fails, repair it within scope or stop; an easier successor does not complete the original task.

Compare competent ordinary methods with equivalent relevant information and account for material differences in computation, tuning, supervision and maintenance. Explain what the ordinary method supplies and which added machinery was unnecessary. When a difference matters, identify evidence for its cause or say the cause is unresolved; use a cheap ablation only if it can change the next decision. Do not invent a mechanism from a leaderboard.

A valid negative remains negative for its original claim. Distinguish that from an instrument failure, inadequate proxy, insufficient evidence, or a useful conventional implementation. A revised question must explain what changed and why it matters independently of rescuing the failed method. Preserve original artifacts and exposed-data status; retrospective interpretation creates no fresh validation.

Retain a cross-domain donor only with a concrete borrowed operation or diagnostic, a receiving question and the assumptions that must transfer. Analogy or shared terminology alone earns no evidence. Useful software, explanation, visualization, fiction, simplification and scoped rejection are legitimate outcomes; no experiment must emerge.

At review, check task fidelity, information boundaries, failures and adverse cases, and whether the claimed lesson follows. Update existing lab records first; update Atlas only for a material change in meaning, constraints, options or routing. Do not automatically start a successor. Existing experiment-specific stops, budgets and protected-evidence rules remain binding.

## Applying the guidance to this repository

For response-interface work, compare the [failed shared age family](experiments/mediated_patterns/EVENT_AGE_OPERATOR_FAMILY.md) with the [local age atlas](experiments/mediated_patterns/EVENT_AGE_OPERATOR_ATLAS.md). Added context resolution can improve interpolation without introducing hidden memory. Keep full-field acquisition, current-center descriptors, known event age, reference baselines and evaluator-only tangents distinct. A future repeated-event or restricted-observer question needs its own information and composition contract; a successful single-event contextual approximation does not answer it. Read the owning experiment's live limits before running anything.
